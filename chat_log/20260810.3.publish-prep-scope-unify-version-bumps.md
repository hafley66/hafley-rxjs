  broke on the workspace's vite 8.2.1 move (from the earlier origin rebase).

- Per-file sed version bumps. devtool-plugin dep link -> workspace:*.

- Publish sweep uses `pnpm --filter <name> ... publish --no-git-checks --access public`
  over the 7 buildable packages, excluding the 2 build failures.
